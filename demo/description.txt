EarthSynth — Hearing a Changing Planet
The Fourier Collective · NASA Space Apps 2026 · The Earth Information Jukebox

A 28-second demonstration of how EarthSynth recovers values from NASA visualization colors, encodes them as the balance between two fixed tones, checks the generated audio with an FFT, and lets listeners combine or isolate ocean, air and wind. Cinematic listening uses related NASA astronaut photographs, not the pictures being measured.

Measured NASA sources:
Ocean / ECCO2 Perpetual Ocean: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/3912/
Global modeled nitrogen oxides (NO + NO2), July 1, 2024: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5518/
Modeled near-surface wind, October 30, 2026: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5148/

These are frozen snapshots from different dates. The shared sweep is picture progress, not simultaneous weather. Wind is kept in image coordinates. The FFT checks our audio encoding, not NASA model uncertainty. Music is an artistic presentation layer; it does not combine physical units.
